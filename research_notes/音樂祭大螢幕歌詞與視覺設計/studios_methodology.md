# How professional concert/festival screen-content studios and show designers build LED visuals from an artist's identity (2018–2026)

Scope note: About 25 searches and fetches. Primary sources are studio project pages (Silent Partners, Rhizomatiks, Sila Sveta), vendor case studies (Disguise, Notch, PLSN), and Taiwanese and Japanese press interviews. Vendor case studies are promotional but name the people and roles involved. I found nothing citable for Moment Factory, 1WAY, teamLab, 簡單生活節 or 浪人祭 screen crews, or for specific budgets. See the Gaps sections.

## 1. What is the typical creative process (brief → moodboard → song-by-song storyboard → content → previs → rehearsal)?

### Takeaway
The steps are the same at every level. It starts with a conversation with the artist or creative director about the show's aim. The team then settles on one overarching concept or world, often split into acts. Styleframes go to the artist for approval, followed by per-song content production and a render-and-revise loop. The work is programmed in a media server (Disguise, Notch) with previs, then adjusted in rehearsal. Big tours spend about 1–2 months on content. Taiwanese solo concerts can finish VJ content in about a month.

### Cited Findings
- Willie Williams (co-founder of Treatment Studio; U2, Rolling Stones): "It always begins with conversations... it is always about understanding the aim of a show and seeing how my contribution can become part of the greater vision." He puts ideas ahead of technology and treats light and video as storytelling tools. — [Square Mile](https://squaremile.com/culture/music/willie-williams-treatment-studio/)
- Treatment Studio was founded by Willie Williams and Sam Pattinson. Their partnership began when Pattinson produced video material for Williams's 2002 Rolling Stones tour. Clients include The Rolling Stones. — [Treatment Studio About](https://www.treatmentstudio.com/about/)
- Es Devlin starts with drawing and reference research, then holds sketching sessions with her studio. The team iterates with 3D models and sculptural studies, and Devlin sketches over printed computer models. — [IIDA Designer Deep Dive](https://iida.org/articles/designer-deep-dive-es-devlin)
- Es Devlin analyses an artist's lyrics as text, and that analysis shapes her stage design. — [Radio Milwaukee](https://radiomilwaukee.org/story/arts-culture/modern-stage-design-artists/)
- Sila Sveta on Drake's Aubrey & The Three Migos tour (2018, creative director Willo Perron):
  - Preparation took about two months before the first show. The concept changed so much by opening night that it was "like two different shows."
  - Styleframes and stills went to the creative director and the artist for approval.
  - Animation was done in Cinema 4D, After Effects, 3ds Max and Blender.
  - Render time was "one of the longest parts of this process."
  - "We developed more than 100 looks for this show." — [Highsnobiety](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/silva-sveta-drake-tour-stage-interview/)
- Willo Perron: "For Drake's tour we collaborated with Sila Sveta to create video content. The result was evolving graphics and effects for each individual song that created a dynamic and rich visual story throughout the show." — [search summary quoting Perron; see Sila Sveta project page](https://www.silasveta.com/projects/drakes-tour)
- FRAY Studio on Laufey's A Matter of Time Tour (with Creative Director Junia Lin and Stufish):
  - The show was split into four acts so the story flowed continuously instead of as isolated moments.
  - Because the set (arches, staircases, a swing) hid parts of the LED wall, video worked as scenography.
  - There were over 2.5TB of content.
  - Disguise "patches" let the team re-render only the changed frames during frequent revisions: "render and replace 100 frames versus all 2000" (Hanna Schrage). — [Disguise case study](https://www.disguise.one/en/insights/case-study/laufeys-matter-time-tour-delivers-theatrical-live-performance-disguise)
- Silent Partners Studio (Montreal, founded 2015) produced the video content for Harry Styles: Live On Tour. Credits show how the work is split:
  - Directors: Baz Halpin and Molly Hawkins. Baz Halpin also did lighting design.
  - Video content producer and lead Notch designers: Silent Partners Studio.
  - Screen producer: J.T. Rooney. Disguise programmer: Lucy Ockenden.
  - Outside video collaborators: Baillat and Lightborne. Illustration: Toma Vagner. Photography: Helene Pambrun. — [Silent Partners project page](https://silentpartnersstudio.com/projects/harry-styles-live-on-tour)
- Taiwan, VJ 小夏 (visuals for Sodagreen tours and the band Vast & Hazy):
  - A large solo concert takes about one month from first meeting to finished content.
  - The first step is meetings with the artist, director and lighting designer to set direction.
  - She then studies the songs and lyrics closely: "必須熟聽歌曲與細看歌詞，從旋律跟文字去想像畫面". — [添翼月刊 NO.45](https://www.team-ear.com/bla_detail.php?id=2334)
- Taiwan, 必應創造 (Mayday's production company):
  - Concerts are treated as "大家集體創作" (collective creation) built around "藝人的本質" (the artist's essence).
  - Lead singer Ashin (阿信) takes part in visual, stage and even video-editing decisions.
  - Meetings run "從下午5點討論到凌晨5點".
  - The company had about 300 staff at its 2018 listing and now runs 7 production departments. — [鏡週刊](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20230530ent028)
- A 必應創造 Facebook post (snippet only, not fetched in full) describes the working rule as "我們習慣先把畫面想清楚再決定哪些東西要出現". It adds that stage, lighting, video and content are never finished separately and are all built within the same rhythm. — [必應創造 FB](https://www.facebook.com/binliveconcert/posts/1433518811894403/)

### Inferences
- A practical pipeline is: artist conversation → show concept or "world" plus act structure → per-song styleframes approved by the artist → content production → media-server programming and previs → rehearsal revisions. Expect the content to change a great deal before opening night.
- Tools that re-render only part of a clip (Disguise patching) and real-time engines (Notch) are how studios cope with late creative changes.

### Gaps
- I found no published timelines broken into phases (for example, weeks of previs versus weeks of rehearsal) for any named studio.
- I did not fetch any Moment Factory, 1WAY or Blink methodology pages. Blink's site lists services only: filmed content, Notch + IMAG, screen visuals, VFX. — [BLINK Inc.](https://www.blink-inc.com/)

## 2. How are band identity elements (album art, logos, lyrics, colour palettes, IMAG) built into the visuals?

### Takeaway
Leading studios make the album world, the artist's hometown or persona, and lyric imagery into a set of visual rules for the whole show. They increasingly composite live camera (IMAG) into the content with custom LUTs and mattes, instead of showing a plain video feed. Taiwanese designers usually pull concrete images from lyrics and design around the emotion of the music.

### Cited Findings
- Feid's Ferxxocalipsis Tour 2024 (video by Luke Halls Studio, stage design by Stufish):
  - The show opens in "a metropolis inspired by Feid's own Medellín, stylised with towering green skyscrapers and flashing neon to match the singer's style." Green is Feid's signature colour.
  - It then moves into surreal worlds of his creative universe.
  - During "Yandel 150," Notch Face Tracker produced real-time infrared imagery with one camera on Feid and one on the audience. A red crosshair bounced between audience faces.
  - The team handled 30 unique screens. — [Notch](https://www.notch.one/madewithnotch/feid-tour-2024)
- Olivia Dean, The Art of Loving tour (FRAY Studio with 1826 Studio):
  - Six upstage projectors and two front-of-house projectors put live camera onto pleated drapes instead of side IMAG screens.
  - Custom LUTs and Notch blocks give each song its own cinematic grade. The system runs on Disguise GX 3 servers.
  - Camera moments between Dean and her backing vocalists were choreographed around key harmonies.
  - Kira O'Brien (video designer): "By choreographing moments with the camera director and lighting designer, we ensured the emotion and energy on stage was captured and reflected in the projection in real time." — [PLSN](https://plsn.com/featured/fray-studio-and-disguise-drive-cinematic-live-camera-projection-for-olivia-deans-the-art-of-loving-tour/)
- Laufey tour: live camera feeds were composited into the content with grading and mattes so IMAG "sits naturally within the visuals." — [Disguise](https://www.disguise.one/en/insights/case-study/laufeys-matter-time-tour-delivers-theatrical-live-performance-disguise)
- Glass Animals' Tour of Earth (2024) is listed as a FRAY Studio project with IMAG and live camera, and its visuals follow the album's space theme. — [Notch](https://www.notch.one/madewithnotch/glass-animals-tour-of-earth)
- The Weeknd, After Hours til Dawn (Es Devlin):
  - The album's dark, post-apocalyptic city mood became a ruined Toronto skyline of 17 scenic buildings.
  - A 30-foot inflatable moon sat in the centre, with a 55m × 15m video screen behind.
  - A robot by Japanese illustrator Hajime Sorayama was added for the second leg. — [Es Devlin](https://esdevlin.com/work/the-weeknd-after-hours-til-dawn-stadium-tour); [TAIT](https://www.taittowers.com/work/after-hours-til-dawn-tour)
- Es Devlin, Pet Shop Boys Pandemonium: cube imagery taken from the album artwork became the main design element. — [Wikipedia: Pandemonium Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandemonium_Tour)
- Rhizomatiks with Perfume at Coachella 2019:
  - OpenPose estimated the three members' poses in real time from camera images.
  - That data was combined with pre-captured 3D scans and a 3D model of the venue.
  - The result was AR masking, morphing and "scribble" effects on the livestream for three songs.
  - This follows Perfume's long-running identity as a technology act. — [Rhizomatiks](https://rhizomatiks.com/en/work/perfume-coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-2019/)
- 傅強 (龐奇視覺設計; Jay Chou, Jolin, Mayday):
  - "音樂所帶動的情緒，是設計重點".
  - He pulls concrete images from lyrics, for example turning Jay Chou lyric images such as "鷹爪、扇子" into animation and stage machinery.
  - With choreographer Travis Payne, Payne gave keywords ("岩漿、蝴蝶、性感") and 傅強 turned them into visuals.
  - About 60% of show time has visuals running. — [La Vie](https://www.wowlavie.com/article/ae1700789)
- VJ 小夏 gave Vast & Hazy a different visual vocabulary for each song:
  - Chaotic monochrome with noise for introspective songs.
  - Bright looks for uplifting songs.
  - Documentary disaster footage for socially critical lyrics. — [添翼月刊](https://www.team-ear.com/bla_detail.php?id=2334)
- aMEI's ASMR concert (visual director 楊士慶): "Most mainstream artist concert visuals focus on the artist's face; this time we wanted audiences to see aMEI while discovering hidden messages". The ear became the core motif. — [VERSE](https://www.verse.com.tw/article/graphic-yang)
- 告五人 (Accusefive):
  - MY MAGIC 宇宙超有趣 tour: nine planets as the stage concept, a 64m LED screen, a 3D spiral structure, 500 drones, and budget above NT$100 million.
  - A later show, 在這裡, used a 100m "time-travel gate" set with photographer and visual artist Miss Bean. The team stated no AI generation was used. — [中時](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20260227002898-260404); [北流公告](https://www.tmc.taipei/tw/news/announcement/ad87367F6B9a)
- Mayday LiFE 人生無限公司 (2017):
  - Designed by LeRoy Bennett / Seven Design Works.
  - 20 spires marked the band's 20th anniversary.
  - 2,400 LED panels allowed 30+ stage configurations across 3,000 m², with a 4K HDR live workflow.
  - 必應 visual lead 陳彥志 described a rocket-launch video timed with real smoke at the moment of launch, turning 2D into 3D. He said, in effect, "not just making images but designing experiences." — [search summary of OCAC / MOT TIMES coverage](https://www.mottimes.com/article/detail/4571)

### Inferences
- The artist's identity usually shows up in three layers:
  1. The show world: hometown, album universe, anniversary symbolism.
  2. Signature colours and motifs: Feid's green, Mayday's 20 spires.
  3. Per-song lyric images.
- Logos appear less often than album-world motifs.
- IMAG is increasingly graded per song to match the content look, rather than shown as neutral broadcast video.

### Gaps
- I found little explicit discussion of lyrics shown on screen as typography at headline shows. One LED vendor blog mentions lyric flashes for sing-alongs in general terms only. — [GoVision](https://govisionxp.com/led-display-technology-live-concerts/)

## 3. How do shows create audience immersion (narrative arc, pacing, call-and-response, sing-alongs, crowd cams)?

### Takeaway
Shows are built as acts or chapters with an arc across the setlist. Crowd cameras and face-tracking make the audience part of the picture. The designers' own view is that visuals should support the music ("a wall behind it") and should not compete with it.

### Cited Findings
- Laufey: four acts for a continuous narrative. — [Disguise](https://www.disguise.one/en/insights/case-study/laufeys-matter-time-tour-delivers-theatrical-live-performance-disguise)
- Feid: the setlist moves from a Medellín-inspired city into surreal abstraction. Crowd-cam face tracking puts fans on screen during "Yandel 150". — [Notch](https://www.notch.one/madewithnotch/feid-tour-2024)
- Es Devlin sees the audience as part of the ritual, "calibrating the role of audience as protagonist". She designs stages to be photographed from every angle, with audiences as "participants in a sculptural portrait". — [Art Basel](https://www.artbasel.com/news/artist-stage-designer-es-devlin-each-piece-has-to-be-a-prototype?lang=en); [Radio Milwaukee](https://radiomilwaukee.org/story/arts-culture/modern-stage-design-artists/)
- Olivia Dean: camera shots choreographed around harmonies bring intimacy "no matter their seat". — [PLSN](https://plsn.com/featured/fray-studio-and-disguise-drive-cinematic-live-camera-projection-for-olivia-deans-the-art-of-loving-tour/)
- An IMAG director cuts a "line cut" that tells the musical story, for example showing each instrument as it comes in. Heavy motion graphics can hide the live action, while "simple colour treatments and mild FX" work well on the field screens. — [Festival Insights](https://www.festivalinsights.com/2015/10/imag/) (published 2015, older than scope but still practical guidance)
- 小夏: "MV的發想比較是『故事性』為主，VJ比較偏向是給予一個氛圍，建造一個環境在舞台上". Also: "我的影像是他們的牆，在背後默默給一道支撐". — [添翼月刊](https://www.team-ear.com/bla_detail.php?id=2334)
- Jolin PLAY (2015, 傅強) used 20 fast-changing scenes to build tension. 梁靜茹's show used a ring LED with animated butterflies for immersion. — [La Vie](https://www.wowlavie.com/article/ae1700789)
- Drake 2018: BlackTrax plus Notch tracked Drake's position so visuals and the LED floor followed him. This was paired with 3D "gags" such as a drone Ferrari. — [Highsnobiety](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/silva-sveta-drake-tour-stage-interview/); [Pin-Up](https://pinupmagazine.org/articles/interview-sila-sveta-drake-scorpion-3d-visuals-scorpion)
- 告五人's 北流 exhibition picked three signature songs (20 minutes) to recreate the immersive concert experience. — [北流](https://www.tmc.taipei/tw/news/announcement/ad87367F6B9a)

### Inferences
- Common pacing tools are: act structure; dense scene changes for high-energy songs versus restrained atmosphere for ballads; tracked or crowd-cam moments at peaks; and physical tie-ins (smoke, drones, pyro) timed with screen "reveals", as in Mayday's rocket.

### Gaps
- I found no published guidance from a named designer on sing-along lyric display or call-and-response screen cues.

## 4. How does festival production differ from a headline tour?

### Takeaway
Festival sets reuse tour content through the artist's own media server, adapted to the festival's screen layout and IMAG, with little rehearsal time on the real LED. The festival's own video crews mainly handle IMAG, livestream and after-movies. Ambitious festival moments, like Perfume at Coachella, are made as a few special segments rather than a full show.

### Cited Findings
- Artists regularly bring their tour media servers to feed festival screens. Fragmented or non-16:9 festival screen layouts force content to be adapted. Rock Werchter used a 16:9 IMAG picture-in-picture in the middle, with LED above and below for graphics. — [Festival Insights](https://www.festivalinsights.com/2015/10/imag/)
- Festival VJ teams take artist content in advance or at check-in and reformat it for festival systems. The advice is to run about a minute of each headliner's visuals on the actual wall during load-in. Media server rental (d3/Disguise, Watchout) costs "several thousand dollars" plus an operator. — [Ticket Fairy blog](https://ticketfairy.com/blog/2025/07/09/video-screens-and-live-streaming-integration-in-festivals) (promoter blog, moderate reliability)
- Rhizomatiks at Coachella 2019 limited the AR to three songs on the livestream, on two weekends (14 and 21 April). The outdoor setting made AR harder. — [Rhizomatiks](https://rhizomatiks.com/en/work/perfume-coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-2019/)
- Perfume took a touring package ("CODE OF PERFUME") to Primavera Sound 2023, again with Rhizomatiks. — [Rhizomatiks](https://rhizomatiks.com/en/work/perfume-live-2023-europe/)
- Fuji Rock's video team has 7 core freelance cinematographers plus 2, led by director 藤井大輔, and includes a drone operator. Planning starts in January. Editing begins on site and runs 1–2 weeks after the festival. Not all headliners allow filming; the 2017 after-movie had no headliner footage. — [fujirockers.org](https://fujirockers.org/?p=13398)
- 大港開唱: the operations team formed from 2017 around ChthoniC drummer Dani and has recruited film-industry talent. Stages have their own themes, such as the 藍寶石 stage modelled on Kaohsiung's cabaret-hall culture and the 大雄丸 floating stage. — [Blow 吹音樂](https://blow.streetvoice.com/66543/)

### Inferences
- For festival sets, plan for: a "festival cut" of the tour content that fits a shared wall and a PiP IMAG layout; minimal or no rehearsal on the real screens; and a line-check of the content during load-in or changeover. Keep the concept simple and legible from far away.
- Tour content often plays full-frame on a custom set. Festival content has to survive being cropped and rearranged.

### Gaps
- I found no first-hand accounts of how Summer Sonic, 大港開唱, 簡單生活節 or 浪人祭 screen crews handle artist content handoff, lyric overlays or house IMAG looks. Japanese searches turned up only generic VJ vendors.

## 5. What are the team roles, rough budgets and case studies?

### Takeaway
A typical headline team is: creative director and show director; production and stage designer; lighting designer; video content studio (creative director, lead designer, animators, Notch artists); screen producer; media-server (Disguise) programmer; camera director. Specific per-song content budgets are not published. The only figures available are total-production numbers and generic animation rates.

### Cited Findings
- Roles on Harry Styles: directors, lighting designer, video content producer, lead Notch designers, screen producer, Disguise programmer, outside animation houses, illustrator, photographer. — [Silent Partners](https://silentpartnersstudio.com/projects/harry-styles-live-on-tour)
- Roles on Laufey (FRAY): video creative director (Finn Ross), lead designer, Notch artist, a four-person animation team, separate US and Europe/Asia Disguise programmers, hardware supplier (Solotech). — [Disguise](https://www.disguise.one/en/insights/case-study/laufeys-matter-time-tour-delivers-theatrical-live-performance-disguise)
- Roles on Olivia Dean: video designer, stage designer, camera director, media servers. — [PLSN](https://plsn.com/featured/fray-studio-and-disguise-drive-cinematic-live-camera-projection-for-olivia-deans-the-art-of-loving-tour/)
- Rhizomatiks credits separate out: creative and technical director (Daito Manabe), main software engineer, video engineer, creative coder, hardware designer, motion capture, and photogrammetry (Avatta). — [Rhizomatiks](https://rhizomatiks.com/en/work/perfume-coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-2019/)
- Sila Sveta: "Big music tour productions cost millions." — [Highsnobiety](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/silva-sveta-drake-tour-stage-interview/)
- 告五人's MY MAGIC production cost more than NT$100 million in total. — [中時](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20260227002898-260404)
- Generic animation rates, from vendor blogs only and not concert-specific:
  - About US$2,000 per minute for custom indie animation.
  - US$15k–70k for a 3-minute 2D music video.
  - US$30k–90k+ for 3D. — [MicahBuzan](https://www.micahbuzan.com/custom-animation/); [Pixune](https://pixune.com/blog/animated-music-video-cost/)
- In Taiwan, 必應創造 describes itself as full-service (planning → visual planning → staging → on-site execution) and makes "專屬的視覺影像企劃" for each artist. — [必應創造 via search / wiki](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E5%BF%85%E6%87%89%E5%89%B5%E9%80%A0)

### Inferences
- A rough inference only, not sourced: if indie animation runs about $2k/min and 3D music-video-grade work runs $10k–30k/min, then 20–25 songs of bespoke tour content would cost from the low hundreds of thousands of US dollars up to seven figures at stadium level. This fits "costs millions" for the whole production, but no studio has confirmed a per-song figure.
- A festival-only package that reuses tour content mainly adds reformatting, a media-server rental (several thousand dollars) and an operator.

### Gaps
- No studio publishes rate cards or per-song content fees. Trade outlets (TPi, Live Design) rarely print content budgets, and TPi was not reachable in full through search.
- Moment Factory, 1WAY, teamLab concert collaborations and the 五月天 / 告五人 content subcontractors were not documented at the methodology level in the sources found. The only named Taiwanese people are 陳彥志, 周佑洋, 馮建彰 and 傅強.
